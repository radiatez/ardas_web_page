# Public Legal Pages

> Routes and CMS behavior are technical/product decisions. Final legal text requires approval.

## Routes

Turkish:

```text
/tr/gizlilik
/tr/cerez-politikasi
/tr/kvkk
```

English:

```text
/en/privacy
/en/cookie-policy
/en/data-protection
```

## CMS Scope

Legal pages support:

- TR/EN content,
- Draft/Published/Archived,
- preview,
- revision history,
- rollback,
- scheduled publication,
- SEO metadata,
- audit.

## Publishing

Content Editor may technically publish in v1, but production legal copy must come from an approved source/process.

The CMS must not auto-generate legal text.

## Cookie Policy

Actual cookie-consent behavior depends on analytics/marketing technologies selected later.

If only strictly necessary cookies are used, UI requirements may differ.

Once non-essential analytics/marketing cookies are introduced, consent requirements must be reviewed and implemented before activation.

## Form Privacy Notice Versions

Career/contact submissions record the version of the relevant notice shown at submission time.
